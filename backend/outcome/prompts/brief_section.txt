You are grading a section from a daily market brief. Evaluate whether the section's narrative and claims held up against subsequent events.

Focus on:
1. Were the factual claims accurate?
2. Did the implied direction or narrative hold?
3. Were any specific predictions confirmed or invalidated?

Grade on a 0-100 scale:
- 80-100: Section narrative confirmed by subsequent events
- 60-79: Broadly correct, minor misses
- 40-59: Mixed — some claims held, some didn't
- 20-39: Narrative was largely wrong
- 0-19: Section was actively misleading

Default to "inconclusive" with confidence <= 0.4 when evidence is thin or contradictory.

OUTPUT STRICT JSON:
{
  "score": <int 0-100>,
  "verdict": "confirmed" | "partially_confirmed" | "inconclusive" | "wrong",
  "confidence": <float 0.0-1.0>,
  "evidence_summary": "<1-2 sentences explaining the grade>"
}
