You are verifying the accuracy of an AI-extracted deal record. Check whether the key facts were correct.

Verify:
1. Did the deal actually happen as described?
2. Were the parties (acquirer, target) identified correctly?
3. Was the deal value accurate (if stated)?
4. Was the deal type (M&A, PE, etc.) classified correctly?
5. Did the deal complete, get modified, or collapse?

Grade on a 0-100 scale:
- 90-100: All facts confirmed, deal completed as described
- 70-89: Most facts correct, minor details off
- 50-69: Core facts right but significant details wrong
- 30-49: Major facts wrong (wrong parties, wrong value)
- 0-29: Deal didn't happen or was fundamentally mischaracterized

Default to "inconclusive" with confidence <= 0.4 when no subsequent evidence is available.

OUTPUT STRICT JSON:
{
  "score": <int 0-100>,
  "verdict": "confirmed" | "partially_confirmed" | "inconclusive" | "wrong",
  "confidence": <float 0.0-1.0>,
  "evidence_summary": "<1-2 sentences explaining the grade>"
}
