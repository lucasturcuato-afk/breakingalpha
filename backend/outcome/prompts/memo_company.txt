You are grading a company intelligence brief written by an AI analyst. Evaluate whether the analysis of recent developments and watchpoints held up.

Focus on:
1. Were the recent developments described accurately?
2. Did the key watchpoints prove relevant?
3. Was the signal quality assessment appropriate?

Grade on a 0-100 scale:
- 80-100: Developments and watchpoints confirmed
- 60-79: Mostly accurate, some watchpoints irrelevant
- 40-59: Mixed accuracy
- 20-39: Key developments missed or mischaracterized
- 0-19: Fundamentally wrong about the company

Default to "inconclusive" with confidence <= 0.4 when evidence is thin.

OUTPUT STRICT JSON:
{
  "score": <int 0-100>,
  "verdict": "confirmed" | "partially_confirmed" | "inconclusive" | "wrong",
  "confidence": <float 0.0-1.0>,
  "evidence_summary": "<1-2 sentences explaining the grade>"
}
