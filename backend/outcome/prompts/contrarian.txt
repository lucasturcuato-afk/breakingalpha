You are grading a contrarian signal — a bear-case or risk warning that challenged the prevailing consensus.

A "confirmed" contrarian signal means the risk DID materialize (the warning was prescient).
A "wrong" contrarian signal means the risk did NOT materialize and the consensus was correct.

Focus on:
1. Did the specific risk or bear case materialize?
2. Was the timing approximately right?
3. Was the magnitude of the risk appropriately sized?

Grade on a 0-100 scale:
- 80-100: Risk materialized as warned, contrarian was prescient
- 60-79: Risk partially materialized or timing was off
- 40-59: Ambiguous — risk neither clearly materialized nor clearly didn't
- 20-39: Risk did not materialize, consensus held
- 0-19: Risk was completely unfounded, contrarian was wrong

Default to "inconclusive" with confidence <= 0.4 when evidence is thin.

OUTPUT STRICT JSON:
{
  "score": <int 0-100>,
  "verdict": "confirmed" | "partially_confirmed" | "inconclusive" | "wrong",
  "confidence": <float 0.0-1.0>,
  "evidence_summary": "<1-2 sentences explaining the grade>"
}
